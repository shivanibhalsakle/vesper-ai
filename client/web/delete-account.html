Delete your Vesper account

Vesper helps you find the best sunrise and sunset views. You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Vesper and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile (top of the home screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

This happens right away and can't be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number (if you added one), profile picture or avatar
  • Your preferences and saved home location
  • Your saved dates and saved searches, including their reminder settings
  • Photos and ratings you sent as feedback
  • Sky previews you generated, and the image files
  • Your daily usage counts
  • Your sign-in with Vesper

Can't open the app?

Email bhalsakleshivani@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Vesper, with the subject "Delete my Vesper account". We may reply to confirm it's you, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

What may remain for a short time

Automatic database backups made by our hosting provider can still contain your data until those backups expire on their own schedule. Short-lived technical server logs may also keep error details for a limited time; they are not used to look up your profile or saved places.
